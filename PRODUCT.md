# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Health Pilot is for individuals who want a lightweight personal health manager for fat-loss and day-to-day wellness tracking. Users are often logging food, weight, exercise, screenshots, or quick notes in the middle of daily life, not sitting down to fill out a formal dashboard. They need the app to accept messy natural language and images, remember context over time, and turn small records into useful guidance without adding friction.

## Product Purpose

Health Pilot is a personal health companion that uses a Doubao-style mobile chat experience as the primary interaction model. The product helps users build and maintain a health profile, record meals and exercise, track weight trends, understand daily calorie and nutrient progress, and receive proactive reminders or nudges when behavior patterns need attention.

Success looks like a user feeling that recording health data is as quick as sending a message, while still getting structured tracking, memory, and practical coaching from the system.

## Brand Personality

Efficient, intelligent, lightweight.

The interface should feel quick to start, easy to scan, and quietly capable. It should borrow the familiar rhythm of modern mobile AI chat, but the product voice belongs to a personal health manager: warm enough for daily use, precise enough for tracking, and restrained enough to be trusted.

## Anti-references

- Do not look like a generic SaaS landing page with purple-blue gradients, gold accents, oversized metrics, or decorative gradient cards.
- Do not feel like a hospital admin system, insurance portal, or medical record backend.
- Do not feel like a high-pressure fitness challenge app that shames the user or over-celebrates streaks.
- Do not copy Doubao proprietary brand assets; reuse the mobile chat interaction pattern, not the brand identity.
- Do not use heavy dashboards as the primary experience when a conversational task flow would be faster.

## Design Principles

1. **Chat first, structure underneath.** The user should feel like they are talking naturally, while the system quietly turns messages, images, and screenshots into structured health records.
2. **Lightweight by default.** Every screen should reduce recording friction. If a user can finish a health task in one message or one tap, prefer that over a form.
3. **Trust through clarity.** Health suggestions must distinguish estimation from fact, ask follow-up questions when data is missing, and avoid pretending to diagnose.
4. **Proactive, not pushy.** Reminders and nudges should feel timely and useful, never guilt-driven or noisy.
5. **Familiar mobile AI patterns.** Reuse the proven Doubao-style chat shell, composer, chips, side navigation, and modal rhythm where it helps users move faster, but adapt content and visual identity to Health Pilot.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Do not rely on color alone for health status or action availability. Support reduced-motion expectations by keeping transitions short and state-driven. Copy should avoid shame, blame, and diet-culture pressure; the product should support users who are returning after missed records or imperfect days.
